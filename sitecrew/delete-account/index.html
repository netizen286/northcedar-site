Delete your SiteCrew account

App: SiteCrew, by NorthCedar Software

How to delete your account

In the app (fastest):

  1. Open SiteCrew.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. Your sign-in is deleted right away and you are signed out.

By email (if you no longer have the app):

  1. Email support@northcedarsoftware.com from the email address you use to sign in to SiteCrew.
  2. Subject: Delete my SiteCrew account.
  3. We may ask you to confirm with a one-time code sent to that address, so we know the request is really from you.
  4. We complete the deletion within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: email address, and phone number if used. You can no longer sign in.
  • Your sign-in security records
  • Data saved on your phone (removed when you sign out or uninstall)

What may be kept, and why

  • Your work time records are kept for your employer, with your name on them. They are part of your employer's payroll records (your name, hours, breaks, projects, notes, punch locations and pay rate). Employers must keep these records for several years by law, for example 4 years in British Columbia, 6 years for the Canada Revenue Agency, 2 to 3 years in the US, 6 years in the UK and New Zealand, and 7 years in Australia. Your employer can still see and export them. They are deleted when your employer deletes them or closes the company, and no later than 7 years after the date of each record.
  • If you are a company owner: deleting your account also deletes your company, and your crew loses access to it. So owners start deletion as a request: in the app go to Settings → Delete account → Email deletion request (it opens a ready-made email to support@northcedarsoftware.com), or email us directly. Export the hours you must keep first. We confirm with you by email and finish within 30 days.
