SiteCrew Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

SiteCrew is a time clock app for construction crews, made by NorthCedar Software ("we", "us"). This policy explains what SiteCrew collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Contact: support@northcedarsoftware.com

1. Who uses SiteCrew

  • Employers (company owners) create a company, invite their crew, see hours and export timesheets.
  • Workers join a company with a team code or invite, and clock in and out.

The employer decides how their company uses SiteCrew. For a worker's time records, the employer is responsible for its own use of that data (for example payroll). We store and process it for them.

2. What we collect

Data Why
Email address (and phone number, if sign-in by phone is used) To sign you in with a one-time code
Name So your employer and crew can see who is who
Company details (company name, time zone, currency, week start day) To run the company account and calculate hours
Work time records: clock-in and clock-out times, breaks, project, notes you add, reasons for hand-entered times, approval status, and changes your employer makes The core purpose of the app: tracking hours worked
Location (latitude, longitude and accuracy) only at the moment you clock in, clock out or switch project, and only if you allow location So your employer can see where a punch happened and to suggest the nearest project. SiteCrew does not track your location at any other time, in the background or after work. You can refuse location and still use the app. If you are an owner, you can also tap "Use my location" when pinning a job site on the map; that reading is saved only as the project's map pin
Phone clock check: the time your phone sent a punch, when our server received it, the difference between your phone's clock and the server's clock, a reading of the phone's "time since it was switched on" counter, and the phone's restart count (how many times it has been switched on, not linked to anything else), when the punch was taken and when it was sent To keep time records accurate and to flag punches made with a wrong phone clock, also when the punch was saved offline and sent later. Original times are never changed silently
Pay rates (entered by the employer) To calculate labour cost and pay before taxes and deductions
Sign-in security records: one-time codes (stored in protected form), number of codes sent and failed attempts To prevent abuse and limit how many sign-in emails are sent

We do not collect: advertising IDs, contacts, photos (not in this version), browsing history, or payment card details. SiteCrew has no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

3. Data stored on your phone

To work without signal, SiteCrew keeps on your phone: your sign-in session (in the phone's secure storage), punches not yet sent, and a copy of your company's projects and crew list. This is removed when you sign out. To work offline, SiteCrew also stores a local copy of the pay rates your account is allowed to view.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your employer (the owner of the company you join) can see your name, email, time records, notes, punch locations and, if set, your pay rate.
  • You can see your own hours. Whether workers see pay is the employer's choice.
  • Other workers in your company cannot see your name, hours, locations or pay. Each worker sees only their own records.
  • Service providers that run SiteCrew for us, only to provide the service:
    • Supabase (database and sign-in), data stored in Canada (Central), AWS region ca-central-1.
    • Resend (sends sign-in code emails), may process your email address in the United States.
    • Google Play and Apple App Store / TestFlight (app distribution).
  • Maps: when SiteCrew shows a map (a punch location, or a job site), your phone downloads the map pictures directly from OpenStreetMap's map servers (OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom). Like any website, those servers receive your phone's IP address, the app's name, and which map area is shown (not your name, email or time records). See the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • Address search: when an owner types a job-site address to find it on the map, the phone's own address service turns it into a map point: Google on Android, Apple on iPhone. The address text you typed is sent to them, under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for advertising.

5. How long we keep data

  • Data is kept while your account or your employer's company is active.
  • When you delete your account, your sign-in, email address and phone number are deleted within 30 days, and you can no longer sign in.
  • Your work time records are kept for your employer, with your name on them. Employers are required by law to keep payroll and time records for several years (for example 4 years in British Columbia under the Employment Standards Act, 6 years for the Canada Revenue Agency, 2 to 3 years under the US Fair Labor Standards Act, 6 years in the UK and New Zealand, and 7 years in Australia). These records include your name, hours, breaks, projects, notes, punch locations and pay rate. Your employer can still see and export them. They are deleted when your employer deletes them or closes their company, and in any case no later than 7 years after the date of the record.
  • Sign-in security records are kept for a short time only: one-time codes expire within minutes, and send counts are kept for up to 30 days.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Location: you can allow or refuse it at any time in your phone settings.
  • Access and correction: ask your employer, or email us.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or on the web: https://northcedarsoftware.com/sitecrew/delete-account. See that page for what is deleted and what is kept.
  • Depending on where you live (for example Canada under PIPEDA or British Columbia's PIPA), you may have other rights. Contact us and we will respond.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to company data is limited by server rules so each company only sees its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

SiteCrew is meant for workers and employers aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell users in the app.
