About the app

This independent study app stores its question bank and practice modes in the app. It does not provide government services. No account is required. The app has no advertising, analytics service, subscription or in-app purchase system.

Local study records

The app saves your study and flashcard position, questions seen, known answers, mistakes to review, topic scores and exam results in local application storage so you can resume learning. These are not immigration application records. NorthCedar Software does not receive these records through the app and operates no app backend or cloud synchronization.

Optional read-aloud

When you choose read-aloud, the app sends displayed question or answer text to your device's text-to-speech service. It does not record your voice or request microphone access. The selected speech engine and voice may download voice data or use an internet service. Their handling depends on their provider and your settings; consult that provider's privacy information. An installed offline voice may support offline speech. Study and exams work without read-aloud.

Storage and external services

Study records use Android's application storage protections. This release disables Android backup for app data and excludes it from the configured backup and device-transfer rules; operating-system or manufacturer behavior can still vary. The app offers no cloud restoration service. Google Play separately processes purchase and installation information under its own terms and policies. An official-source or support link may open an external service, which may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address.

Retention and deletion

Local study records remain until you use Reset progress on the home screen and confirm, clear this app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Reset does not alter purchase records held by Google Play or records independently held by your speech engine or external services. The app has no user account to delete.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use these messages to respond, resolve issues and keep necessary support records. Messages are retained for as long as needed for those purposes and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can ask us about or request deletion of your support correspondence using the contact below; records that must be kept for an applicable obligation may be retained. Do not send passports, application numbers or other immigration documents. Email and website providers may process connection and message information to operate those services; this is separate from the study progress stored in the app.

Contact and updates

Contact NorthCedar Software at support@northcedarsoftware.com for privacy or support questions. We will update this page and its effective date if app practices change. This policy covers the named app; external services have their own policies.
