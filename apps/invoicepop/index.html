Questions, answered honestly

The things people actually ask before trusting an app with their business records.

How do I get paid through InvoicePop?

You don’t — and we say that plainly. InvoicePop does not process payments or hold your money. You invoice with InvoicePop, your client pays you the way you already work (bank transfer, cash, your card reader), and you record the payment in the app so your balances are always right.

Do I need an account?

No. You can create and manage invoices entirely on your device as a guest. An account is only needed if you want cloud sync and account features.

What happens to my voice and AI drafts?

Voice input uses your device’s speech recognition and only runs when you tap the voice button; InvoicePop itself does not save audio. AI drafts are optional: nothing is sent until you confirm, and the confirmation names the AI service used. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account and data?

Any time, by email — you don’t even need the app installed. Full steps are on the account deletion page.
