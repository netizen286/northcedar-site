ClearSpace Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Your media is processed on your device. Support messages and website visits are separate and are explained below.

1. Who provides ClearSpace

ClearSpace is provided by Armin Kelishani through North Cedar Software. This policy covers the ClearSpace Android and iOS apps, ClearSpace support correspondence and the ClearSpace pages on northcedarsoftware.com. App-specific descriptions reflect the reviewed Android 0.7.2 and iOS 0.1.3 releases; features and permissions vary by platform and installed version. For privacy questions or support, email clearspacesupport24@gmail.com. General support is available at https://northcedarsoftware.com/#support.

2. Information processed on your device

ClearSpace reads the media you authorize so you can review storage use, compare exact duplicates and visually similar photos, choose files to remove, and make photo or video copies. Depending on your platform and permissions, this includes media contents, file or asset identifiers, names, types, sizes, folders or albums, dimensions, dates, and available favorite and other media metadata. Similar-photo suggestions are computed on your device.

On Android, authorized photos, videos and audio are accessed through Android's media interfaces. You can also choose an individual photo or video through a system picker. Access to individually picked documents is temporary; ClearSpace does not retain a persistent document permission for them, and you may need to select a file again after reopening the app. Optional Usage Access lets ClearSpace read app names and package identifiers, installation dates, recorded last-use information, and available storage or cache estimates. It does not give ClearSpace access to other apps' private content. Social-app categories use media Android exposes; they do not read private chats.

On iOS, ClearSpace reads the Photos assets and albums allowed by your Photos permission, including a limited selection when you choose limited access. Exact comparison can read the underlying resources of an asset, including Live Photo resources. Social categories match accessible Photos album names; they do not open private social-app storage. The Audio tool reads and plays only a file you explicitly choose through Files.

3. What ClearSpace sends to us

The apps do not send your media library, scan results, media contents, app-usage information or review choices to North Cedar Software or a ClearSpace server. ClearSpace has no account system or advertising and does not operate user-behaviour analytics. On Android 0.7.2, batch presets, the monthly free-use counter and the cached Pro status stay on the device. Opening Batch conversion connects to Google Play to check the current offer and whether this app has an eligible purchase, including when you use a free batch. Google Play processes payments. ClearSpace verifies the signed purchase on the device and sends its purchase token to Google Play to acknowledge a completed purchase. The developer can view order information in Play Console for support and refunds. ClearSpace does not receive your card details. Clearing app data removes the local counter and cached status. Google Play manages the store order history.

The Google Play Billing library can send Google technical events about billing operations even when you do not buy Pro. Its diagnostic transport can attach information such as the app build, Android version, device model and system build, language and region settings, time zone, and network or mobile operator information. This helps diagnose billing problems. ClearSpace does not attach your photos, videos or scan results to billing requests. We do not sell the information processed by these app features.

This does not mean that every system action is offline. Android document providers, Apple's system pickers, iCloud Photos, browsers, email apps and apps you choose to share with can use their own services under their own policies.

ClearSpace's iOS library scans, thumbnails and video-source requests disable automatic iCloud downloading and may skip items unavailable locally. Selecting a photo in the system photo picker can retrieve that photo from iCloud. A file selected through Files or an Android document picker may be supplied by a cloud provider. If you open support, email us, share a copy or open media in another app, the selected app or service handles that action.

4. Local records and temporary files

Android stores app preferences, swipe-review choices and progress, media identifiers used to track its Trash view, and records needed to handle pending operations or incomplete exports in app-private storage. It also uses local caches and temporary export files. Interrupted exports may leave temporary files or incomplete shared-storage entries. Cleanup removes only entries the app can safely identify as its own; it does not guarantee that every leftover is removed after a crash. The Android app is configured to exclude its private app data from cloud backup and device transfer. This setting does not control backups of your media library by Android, your device manufacturer or another app.

On iOS, scan results, comparison information and review selections are held in the current app session. Reading media resources and creating exports can create temporary files in the app's private container. Temporary files are removed in normal completion or replacement paths where implemented; an interrupted operation may leave temporary files until the app or operating system removes them. We do not promise immediate removal of every temporary file after a crash.

Clearing Android app storage or deleting the app removes its app-local records subject to the operating system's behavior. Deleting the iOS app removes its local container. Uninstalling either app does not remove copies already saved to your photo library, files exported outside the app, shared copies, or information held by other apps.

5. Copies, metadata and sharing

Photo conversion and video compression create new copies and do not intentionally overwrite the original. Android saves completed exports to ClearSpace folders in shared Pictures or Movies storage. A video copy that is not smaller than its source, or whose source size cannot be established, remains in private temporary storage until you choose to keep or discard it. Keeping it saves the copy to shared storage; discarding it removes that temporary copy. On iOS, the tools first create a temporary copy; you can choose to save it to Photos or share it.

Android photo exports retain available capture dates and a limited set of camera and author fields. GPS location metadata is removed by default. You can explicitly turn on "Keep location if available" to copy available source location metadata; Android or the document provider may redact it. Camera-specific maker notes and embedded previews are not copied after resizing. The iOS still-image conversion does not deliberately copy the original EXIF/GPS metadata; its output is a still image, including when the selected source is a Live Photo. Video exports and system saving or sharing may retain metadata. An export is not a promise that all metadata has been removed. Review any copy before sharing it.

Once you save or share a copy, the destination app, photo library or provider controls its storage, synchronization and further use.

6. Permissions, deletion and your choices

You can deny or revoke media permissions in system Settings. Limited or selected-photo access restricts what ClearSpace can show. Android audio access is separate from selected-photo access on supported versions. Android Usage Access is optional and can be disabled independently. On iOS, saving a copy to Photos uses the system's photo-library add permission.

You choose the items to remove and confirm the operation. On Android 8–10, confirmed removal is permanent because system Trash is unavailable; gallery favorite protection is also unavailable. On Android 11 and later, Android's media Trash is used where supported. ClearSpace's Trash screen tracks items it placed there; it is not a separate backup. Trash contents may still consume storage until permanently removed or expired by Android.

On iOS, deletion is performed through Photos with the system confirmation. Photos manages Recently Deleted and recovery. If iCloud Photos is enabled, deletion may synchronize to your other devices. ClearSpace does not control that protected recovery album or promise immediate storage recovery.

You can remove saved exports using the relevant Photos, gallery or file-management app. Clearing ClearSpace's local data does not undo media deletion, clear another app's storage, or delete copies you previously shared.

7. Support messages, providers and website visits

Only Armin has access to this support mailbox and reads the messages and attachments. He uses them only to reply, investigate the issue and follow up on the same request. Visiting our website or following a link is handled by your browser and the relevant website. This app policy does not describe all processing by Apple, Google, your device manufacturer, cloud-storage providers, email providers or other apps you choose.

8. Security and children

ClearSpace uses the operating system's media permissions and app-private storage for its own local records. Access also depends on your device security, operating-system settings and the permissions you grant. ClearSpace is a general-purpose media and storage utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through support. If a child has sent personal information to us, a parent or guardian may contact us so we can assess and remove it where appropriate. Store age ratings do not change applicable consent requirements. No security measure is perfect. If an incident affects information in our custody or control, we will assess it and provide any notifications required by applicable law.

9. Retention, disclosures and international processing

Armin keeps messages and attachments for open requests in Gmail until the request is resolved. Within 90 days after a request is closed, he manually deletes its messages and attachments from the mailbox and permanently removes them from Gmail Trash. If the law requires particular records to be kept longer, he keeps those records for the required period.

Google provides Gmail for the support mailbox. GitHub Pages hosts the website. Hosting providers receive technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website. GitHub documents that Pages visitor IP addresses are logged for security. These ClearSpace legal pages do not embed advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels. Provider operations and browser or system services may involve processing outside your province or country, including in the United States, where local laws and lawful government access may differ. See GitHub's privacy statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. Visiting this website does not give us access to your photo library.
