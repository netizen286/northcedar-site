4. Deletion and recovery differ by platform

On Android 8–10, confirmed media removal is permanent because the system Trash facility used by newer Android versions is unavailable. Gallery-favourite protection is unavailable on these older versions; do not rely on a gallery star to prevent removal. On Android 11 and later, supported operations use Android's media Trash. ClearSpace's Trash view tracks relevant items; it is not an independent backup. Android or your gallery may expire or permanently remove trashed items. Recovery availability and storage release depend on the operating system and provider.

On iOS, Photos handles deletion and system confirmation. Apple manages Recently Deleted and any recovery period. If iCloud Photos is enabled, deletion may synchronize across devices. ClearSpace cannot guarantee recovery or immediate free space.

Removing ClearSpace does not restore deleted media. Uninstalling another app can remove that app's data; clearing another app's storage or cache is controlled by Android or the device manufacturer. ClearSpace does not promise access to private messages, private app storage or every cached file.
