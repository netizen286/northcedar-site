Questions, answered honestly

The things people actually ask before trusting an app with their photos.

Will ClearSpace delete my photos automatically?

ClearSpace requires your selection and confirmation before removing media. On Android 8–10, removal is permanent. On Android 11 and later, supported items use system Trash and may expire automatically. On iOS, Photos manages Recently Deleted and iCloud deletion may synchronize across devices. Keep a separate backup; recovery and immediate storage savings are not guaranteed.

Which of my files can it see?

Only the photos, videos and audio you allow. You grant access, you stay in control, and you review everything on your own screen.

Why is my WhatsApp folder so big?

Saved media can accumulate over time. ClearSpace groups accessible media by supported folders or album names; it cannot see everything stored privately inside a messaging app. Categories vary by platform and permissions.

Can it shrink files instead of deleting them?

The tools create resized or compressed copies with supported formats and settings. Savings vary; an output can be larger and compression may affect detail, colour, HDR or metadata. Review the copy before deciding whether to remove the original.
