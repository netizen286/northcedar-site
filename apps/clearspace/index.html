Questions, answered honestly

The things people actually ask before trusting an app with their photos.

Will ClearSpace delete my photos automatically?

No — never. Nothing is selected automatically and nothing is removed until you review it and choose it yourself. Removed items go to ClearSpace’s Trash first, and the space is only reclaimed when you empty it.

Which of my files can it see?

Only the photos, videos and audio you allow. You grant access, you stay in control, and you review everything on your own screen.

Why is my WhatsApp folder so big?

Years of forwarded photos and videos add up quietly. ClearSpace groups media per app — WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok — so you can see exactly what each one is holding and clean it deliberately.

Can it shrink files instead of deleting them?

Yes. The built-in photo tools resize images and convert between JPEG, PNG and WebP, and the video tools compress recordings at a quality you pick — keep the memory, lose the megabytes.
