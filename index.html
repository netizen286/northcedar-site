How we work

Four principles shape every app we make — the first one, and every one after it.

🧭

Simple by design

If a feature needs a manual, it goes back to the drawing board. Our apps do a few things exceptionally well instead of everything halfway.

🔒

Private by default

Your records belong to you. We collect the minimum, we never sell data, and every app ships with a plain-language privacy policy and a real way to delete your account.

🤝

Honest & human

No dark patterns, no surprise charges, no chatbots pretending to be support. When you write to us, a real person reads it and answers.

🌱

Built to last

We build on open standards and keep everything portable, so our apps — and your data — never depend on one platform's mood to keep working.
